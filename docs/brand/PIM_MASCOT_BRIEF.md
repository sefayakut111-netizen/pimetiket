# Pim — Brand Mascot & Logo Design Brief

**Status:** Source of truth for any illustrator, designer, or AI prompt working on Pim's visual identity.
**Last updated:** 2026-05-08
**Owner:** Sefa Yakut

This brief is **the canonical specification** for Pim. The `design-prototype/v1-jsx/pim.jsx` SVG is an
internal placeholder; production logo work (vector source files, app icons, favicons, marketing
illustrations) must conform to this document. See also: [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md §4](../DESIGN_SYSTEM.md)

---

Brand mascot logo design for "Pim Etiket" — a Turkish digital print
shop specializing in product labels (rolled) and stickers (sheet).

================================================================
CHARACTER: Pim
================================================================
A round, egg-shaped **owl** character — stylized, soft, expressive.
Cute, warm, subtly masculine, gender-neutral in feel. Inspired by
the round silhouette of Duolingo's Duo, the illustrated warmth of
Mailchimp's Freddie, and the iconic intellectual aura of Hedwig
(Harry Potter), but visually its own distinct character. Pim is
portrayed as the "professor of labels" — a master craftsman who
inspects every print with care.

The owl species is intentional: owl = wisdom + sharp inspection +
nocturnal eye for detail. This pairs perfectly with the brand's
"Etiket Profesörü" (Label Professor) persona and the magnifier
inspect pose.

================================================================
BODY AND FACE
================================================================
- Egg-shaped body, vertically oriented, soft round silhouette
- Two stylized owl wings on the sides — flat-vector simplification,
  one or two visible feather strokes maximum (NOT realistic plumage),
  slightly darker coral than body
- A subtle V-shape hint on the chest suggesting soft chest plumage —
  one or two short curved strokes only (NOT detailed feathers)
- Two small orange owl talons at the bottom — three short toes per
  foot, simplified geometric shapes (NOT anatomically detailed)
- **Heart-shaped face disc** — a soft, lighter-toned area framing
  both eyes (the signature owl facial structure). Subtle, low
  contrast — read as a face feature, not a separate shape
- Two short ear tufts on top of the head — small triangular
  silhouettes flanking the crown (subtle, optional in simplified
  marks). They give Pim a slightly inquisitive, alert profile
- Large round eyes: white sclera, dark navy pupils, small white
  highlights for life. Eyes are positioned **frontally** (owl-style,
  both eyes face forward — not bird-side-eye), spaced wide enough
  for the round glasses to fit naturally over both
- Short straight eyebrows, slightly thick — subtle masculine cue,
  conveying focus and expertise
- Small **hooked owl beak** in warm orange — a short curved
  triangular shape (NOT a flat songbird triangle); tip bends
  slightly downward, beak overall reads as soft and friendly
  (closer to a cartoon owl than a raptor)
- Subtle soft-pink cheek blush dots at low opacity, placed on the
  face disc area

================================================================
GLASSES
================================================================
- Round, slightly oversized eyeglasses — medium-to-large round
  frames that fully cover both eyes
- Style reference: classic round eyewear (Harry Potter / vintage
  intellectual), but a touch bolder and larger
- Thin-to-medium black frames, full circular shape
- Both eyes fully visible through the round lenses
- Sits on the bridge of the beak
- Conveys: intellectual, characterful, expert

================================================================
COAT
================================================================
- Short workshop master coat / lab coat, waist-length (between a
  chef's coat and a doctor's coat in cut)
- CRITICAL: The coat is clearly OPEN in the front, hanging from
  the shoulders like a garment worn OVER the body — not closed,
  not buttoned, not a jumpsuit
- The coral body of Pim is clearly visible through the open V-neck
  and the front opening
- Two flaps of the coat fall on either side of the front, with the
  coral body showing down the middle of the chest and belly
- V-neck collar visible at the top, opening into the front gap
- Color: warm cream (PANTONE 9221 C) with subtle tan stitching
- Sleeves rolled up to the elbow, showing coral wing tips
- A few faint ink smudges signaling "actively working" — not sterile
- Vibe: workshop master with the spirit of a professor —
  experienced, hands-on, trusted

================================================================
POCKETS (critical brand storytelling)
================================================================
LEFT POCKET — represents the product label business (serious tone):
- A roll of product labels (rulo etiket) sticking out the top
- Roll color: white/cream, slight industrial feel
- One label strip hangs down out of the pocket, swaying gently
- Faint indication of barcode or "ÜRÜN" text on the hanging label
- Orderly, professional appearance

RIGHT POCKET — represents the sticker business (fun tone):
- A colorful collection of stickers spilling out the top
- Mixed shapes: star, heart, droplet, circle (4-5 distinct shapes)
- Mixed vivid colors using the sticker accent palette below
- One sticker is half-peeled, showing the adhesive backing
- One small sticker bears a tiny "P" letter — Pim's own signature
- Joyful, abundant, playful appearance

The two pockets together visually communicate the dual product
lines: "serious product labels AND fun stickers" without words.

================================================================
POSE
================================================================
- Standing upright, slight forward lean
- Right wing raised, holding a small classical magnifying glass
  (brass/gold ring, dark wooden handle, subtle glint on lens),
  tilted up as if examining a print
- Left wing relaxed at side near the left pocket
- Slight wise smile, friendly but expert demeanor

================================================================
ART STYLE
================================================================
- Flat vector illustration
- Bold, confident outlines
- Limited shading: one tone of shadow per area, no gradients on body
- Modern, clean, friendly with professional warmth
- Character must remain readable and recognizable at small sizes
  (favicon 32px, app icon 64px)

================================================================
LOGO COMPOSITION VARIATIONS NEEDED
================================================================

1. DETAILED MASCOT — full Pim with all elements: open coat, round
   glasses, magnifying glass, both pockets with contents. For hero
   illustrations, marketing visuals, character moments, social
   media. This is the "rich" version.

2. SIMPLIFIED LOGO MARK (CRITICAL — for actual logo use) — A
   much cleaner, minimal version of Pim, designed specifically for
   logo placements where complexity loses readability:
   - NO magnifying glass
   - NO pocket contents — pockets are either omitted or reduced to
     simple outlines only
   - Coat may be simplified to a clean V-shape collar hint, or
     omitted entirely (just the bird with glasses)
   - Bold, clean, geometric outlines
   - Maximum 3-4 colors total
   - Designed to remain recognizable at small scales (32px favicon)
   - KEEPS: round egg body, round glasses (signature element),
     beak, eyebrows, friendly expression
   - This simplified mark is what gets used in actual brand
     applications — business cards, app icons, headers

3. ICON ONLY — Just Pim's head and upper shoulders, simplified,
   for favicon, app icon, social media avatars. Round glasses
   must be clearly visible.

4. COMBINATION MARK — Simplified Pim mark + "pim etiket" wordmark
   in modern rounded sans-serif, wordmark in PANTONE 532 C. Use
   the SIMPLIFIED mark, not the detailed mascot.

5. HORIZONTAL LOCKUP — Simplified Pim on the left, wordmark on
   the right.

6. VERTICAL LOCKUP — Simplified Pim on top, wordmark below.

7. SINGLE-COLOR VERSION — Simplified Pim silhouette in solid coral
   OR solid black, for stamps, embroidery, single-color print
   applications. Magnifying glass lens must remain legible (not
   filled solid) so the silhouette doesn't read as a "racket".

================================================================
TYPOGRAPHY (for wordmark)
================================================================
- "pim etiket" set in lowercase
- Modern rounded geometric sans-serif (recommended: Nunito,
  Poppins, or Quicksand)
- Medium weight (500), not bold
- Letter spacing slightly open for friendliness
- Color: PANTONE 532 C (charcoal navy)

================================================================
REFERENCES (for style direction)
================================================================
- Hedwig (Harry Potter) — round owl silhouette + intellectual
  glasses pairing; Pim is friendlier and more illustrated, but
  the "wise owl with eyewear" association is intentional
- Duolingo Duo — round body, expressive face, soft mascot warmth
- Mailchimp Freddie — illustrated warmth, character personality
- Reddit Snoo (older versions) — simple distinctive silhouette
- Modern flat-vector owl mascots: Hootsuite Owly (older), Hooty
  (lightweight illustrated owl), avoiding any realistic raptor
  styling
- Modern flat-vector mascot logos: Slack, Headspace, Notion
  illustrations
- For round glasses reference: classic intellectual eyewear
  (Harry Potter style, but bolder and slightly larger)

================================================================
OUTPUT FORMAT
================================================================
- SVG vector, scalable from 32px to 2000px
- Transparent background (with PNG export fallback)
- Each variation as a separate file or artboard

================================================================
THINGS TO AVOID
================================================================
- 3D rendering, photorealism, embossed effects
- Detailed feather textures (body must read as a smooth shape;
  feathers are HINTED, not drawn)
- **Realistic raptor/predator owl styling** — no sharp talons,
  no menacing brow, no nocturnal-creepy palette. Pim is a friendly
  cartoon owl, not a Halloween owl
- **Side-eye songbird styling** — owl eyes must be FRONTAL, not
  bird-side-eye. This is what makes the glasses fit naturally
- Multiple gradients, neon glows, drop shadows
- Background scenery or props beyond what's described on Pim
- Overly cartoonish proportions (keep the egg shape clean)
- Closed/buttoned coat — coat must read as open and worn over body
- Half-moon glasses — glasses must be round and slightly oversized
- Text/typography mixed inside the character (other than the
  tiny "P" sticker and faint label text)
- Generic "tech bro" mascot styles (no flat geometric robots)
- Substrate-shifting metallics outside the magnifying glass and
  sticker pocket
- Using the DETAILED mascot for combination marks/lockups — those
  must use the SIMPLIFIED logo mark

## Notes for Pim Etiket team

- The current `design-prototype/v1-jsx/pim.jsx` and `storefront/src/components/Pim.tsx`
  SVGs are **humanoid placeholders** (skin tone, human-like body, krem apron). They do NOT
  match this brief. They will be **replaced** when the professional vector owl logo is
  delivered, and the SVG components re-rendered with the same 9 pose API (`wave`, `think`,
  `wait`, `inspect`, `happy`, `sad`, `excited`, `box`, `chat`).
- The 9-pose interface described in `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md §4` survives the redesign — only the
  underlying paths change.
- All product copy referencing "Etiket Profesörü" assumes the **owl-with-glasses** character;
  copy stays unchanged when the visuals are updated. Voice/tone unaffected.
- Brand colors (`#FF6B5B`, `#F5EBD9`, `#1F2937`, `#FF9933`, `#FFA89E`) are already wired into
  `design.css`/`styles.css` tokens and `globals.css` (Tailwind 4 `@theme`). The Pantone
  references above are the **print-side authoritative values** for any physical brand
  collateral (business cards, packaging, stamps, embroidery).
- When commissioning the professional illustrator: this brief is the canonical input.
  Hand it over verbatim. Variations 1-7 (Detailed Mascot, Simplified Logo Mark, Icon Only,
  Combination Mark, Horizontal/Vertical Lockups, Single-Color) are all required deliverables.
